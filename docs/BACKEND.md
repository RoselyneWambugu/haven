# Echo backend

Six slices of the real backend are built on the existing Supabase project `haven` (ref `htsfkotheyrfyhqzeiny`, eu-west-1): **accounts and privacy** (slice 1), **peer matching and chat** (slice 2), **the community forum and support spaces** (slice 3), **plans and billing** (slice 4), **therapists and sessions, the member's side** (slice 5) and **the therapist portal** (slice 6). Group chats, the therapist application and appeals, and the admin dashboard still run on the mock on the device.

## How privacy is kept

- **Two sides.** Anything others may see (pen name, avatar, languages, topics, age range) is in `public.profiles`. Anything private (full name, phone, email, date of birth, ID check) is in `private.identities`, in a schema the app's API cannot reach at all. Old pen names are kept in `private.pen_name_history`.
- **The app never writes tables.** Every change goes through a function that checks the rules on the server (age 18+, pen-name rules, 30-day pen-name limit, allowed settings) and answers `{ ok, code, error }`. Rules hold even if the app is modified.
- **Row-level security on every table.** A member can read only their own rows. Signed-out visitors can read and call nothing.
- **One call to load everything.** `my_account()` returns the member's account in one reply. The app keeps it as a cache and clears it on sign-out.
- Pen names are unique ignoring case. A pen name containing a part (3+ letters) of the member's own full name is refused, server side.

## Peer matching and chat (slice 2)

- **Matching** is on the server. Someone who asks for a match joins a private waiting list; the next person who fits is paired with them. Fit means: shared language, the same age range (a neighbouring one if nobody fits), not blocked either way, under five open chats, and not paired with each other in the last 30 days. The app asks again every 3 seconds while searching.
- **Chats** live in `conversations`, `conversation_members` and `messages`. A member can read only their own chats. The other person is shown by pen name and avatar only; the app never sees their account id, only a per-chat alias.
- **Messages** are written only by `send_message`, which checks the plan hook, length and the contact-details filter. The filter is a port of the app's `detectBlockedContent`; `supabase/tests/chats.sql` checks both agree on 38 sample messages.
- **Chats end after 24 hours** and are deleted by a job that runs every minute (pg_cron), whether or not anyone opens the app. A note is added an hour before. A **reported** chat is not deleted: it is hidden from both people and kept for the safety review.
- **Reports** are written by `report_chat` to a table the app cannot read (`private.reports`). The server keeps the last 10 messages as they were, with the other person's last 3 marked, so editing or deleting afterwards changes nothing.
- **Blocking** ends the chat for both, hides it for the blocker, stops future pairing, and keeps the blocked person's pen name and avatar so they can be shown in the blocked list.
- The app polls every 3 seconds for new messages and matches (Realtime would be the next step). New messages from others create a notification, as in the mock.

## Forum and support spaces (slice 3)

- **Nothing is readable directly.** The posts, comments, supports and space memberships sit in the `private` schema. The app reads through `forum_feed()` and writes through functions, so the plan check and the space rules apply to every read.
- **Who sees what.** Live posts in the general community and in open spaces are visible to signed-in members. A **sensitive** space (domestic violence, PTSD) is visible only to members who joined; leaving removes it from view at once and keeps no record. A member's own post that is still being checked is visible to them alone.
- **Checking before it goes live.** A member's first post, and every post in a sensitive space, is held. Until staff have a review queue, a held post goes live after 15 seconds (`private.auto_approve_after()`). That stands in for a person checking it and must be replaced before real use.
- **Rules on the server:** the contact-details filter on posts and comments, title and body length, topic from the list, joined-space for space posts, 15-minute edit window, at most 10 posts and 60 comments an hour, no commenting on a post that isn't live, paused accounts can't post.
- **Authors** are shown by pen name and avatar. Each author has an opaque key (`profiles.public_key`) so a member can mute them; it is not the account id.
- **Reports** of posts and comments go to `private.reports` with a snapshot of the post, or of the comment with the one before it.
- **Removing a post** deletes it and its comments for good. Deleting an account removes everything that member wrote.
- No starter posts: the real community begins empty. The app asks for the feed every 9 seconds.

## Plans and billing (slice 4)

- **The server now decides who has a plan.** Matching, chat messages, the community and support spaces all check it. A member whose plan has ended can still read their chats (read-only) but nothing else paid. The earlier "plan check on the device only" gap is closed for everything built so far.
- **Plans** (`private.plans`): daily, monthly and yearly, with the same sample prices the app shows (KES 100, 1,500, 12,000). The real prices are still to be decided; changing them is one update to that table and the app picks it up.
- **Checkout** creates a pending order. Paying adds to a plan the member already has, so a second purchase starts when the first ends. A new checkout cancels the previous open one. Cancelling a plan stops renewal but keeps what was paid for.
- **No payment provider is connected.** Two ways in:
  - `confirm_payment(order, provider_ref, method)` is for the provider's webhook. It can only be called with the service role (from an edge function, later), never by the app, and confirming twice changes nothing.
  - `simulate_payment(...)` completes a payment without money, and only for phone numbers in `private.payment_test_phones`. Add a test number like this in the SQL editor: `insert into private.payment_test_phones (phone) values ('+254712345678');`. Everyone else sees "Payments aren't switched on yet." Empty this table before real use.
- **Renewal is a flag only.** Nothing charges a member again automatically; that needs the provider (M-Pesa has no standing pull payments without extra setup).
- **Therapist sessions** are still paid through the mock. They come with the therapists slice.

## Therapists and sessions (slice 5, the member's side)

- **Who the therapists are.** The directory holds six sample therapists (`is_demo`) with no account behind them. They stand in for real, verified therapists until therapist onboarding is built. Nobody signs in as a therapist yet, so **nobody answers a therapist chat** and nobody marks attendance. The member's chat, booking and sessions all work; the therapist's side is the next slice.
- **Directory.** `therapist_directory()` returns each therapist's profile, weekly hours, price, real rating total and count, and the start times already booked by other members (no names). Browsing is free. The rating shows only once there are enough reviews, as before.
- **Booking.** A time can be booked only if it is inside the therapist's hours, in a session type they offer, at least two hours ahead (or within the hour if the therapist is on now), within 14 days, and clear of their other sessions. The server checks this when the checkout opens and again when the payment lands, so two people can't get the same time. If the time is lost during payment the order fails with "taken". If a provider had already taken money, `confirm_payment` answers `refund: true` so the webhook can refund it.
- **Rules kept on the server:** a plan is needed to book; one therapist at a time; a free change or cancel until 24 hours before, a late-change flag after; a session can be joined from 10 minutes before until it ends; a session can be finished only after it starts; a session is rated once it has finished, and the comment stays private.
- **Payment** uses the same orders and test-number path as plans (see Plans and billing). A paid session creates the session, and for a chat session the therapist chat.
- **Therapist chats** are conversations of kind `therapist`. They last a week from the start, or until a day after the latest booked or finished session, and get an "ends in about a day" note. They stay open after a plan lapses. Blocking and reporting a person are for peer chats; a concern about a therapist goes through `report_session`, which keeps the session details and the last messages, goes to the clinical queue, and keeps the chat for review.
- **Calls** are not on the server. Joining checks the time window; the call itself is still simulated on the device (backlog S-05).

## The therapist portal (slice 6)

- **Signing in.** A therapist signs in with a phone number and a code, like a member. Echo staff link the number to a therapist record. Until there is an admin screen for it, do it in the SQL editor, after that number has signed in once:
  `update private.therapists set account_id = (select id from auth.users where phone = '254712345678') where id = 't-amina';`
  A number that isn't linked is signed out again. A person can be a member or a therapist on one device at a time: signing in to the portal signs the member app out, and the other way round.
- **What a therapist sees.** `my_portal()` returns their schedule, sessions, inbox, concerns and payout setting in one reply. Clients appear only as a pen name and an avatar. The therapist's own account is never shown to members.
- **Schedule.** Weekly hours, pausing new clients, time off, and limits (sessions a day and a buffer between sessions) are enforced on the server when members book. Time off tells each member who has a session in it, in their therapist chat. "New clients a week" is stored but not enforced yet.
- **Sessions.** A therapist can join from 10 minutes before the start, keep private notes that only they can read, and wrap up with attended, late or no-show. A no-show is not paid. Earnings are worked out in the app from finished sessions at the placeholder 15% fee.
- **Inbox.** A therapist reads and answers their clients' chats. Messages go through the same contact-details filter. A therapist can close a thread or offer a time, which tells the client to book it from Echo.
- **Forum.** A therapist reads the community without a plan and replies under their own name. Replies can't invite people to book or make contact (checked on the server).
- **Concerns** a therapist raises about a client are stored for the on-call clinical lead. There is no screen for them yet.
- **Payout.** Changing it needs a code sent to the therapist's phone, checked on the server: the sign-in must be under five minutes old.
- **Still on the mock:** applying to join Echo and licence checks, appeals against a suspension or review, and the clinical reviewer's decisions.

## The admin dashboard (slice 7)

Migration `20261021000700_admin.sql`, checks in `supabase/tests/admin.sql`.

- **Staff sign in with their phone**, like everyone else, but only a number linked to a row in `private.staff` gets in. An Admin links a number with SQL for now (insert the staff member's auth account, a name of 3 to 30 characters, and a role: moderator, clinical, billing or admin). A member signed in on the same device is signed out, and the other way round.
- **Cases** are the member reports and the therapist concerns already stored. `admin_state()` returns what the role may see: moderators see member reports, clinical reviewers and Admins also see therapist concerns, billing sees neither. Content is only returned to staff, and opening a case is written to the audit log.
- **Rules enforced on the server.** A decision needs a reason. Strikes follow the ladder (warning, restriction, suspension; Severity 1 goes straight to suspension). Only moderators and Admins apply sanctions; clinical reviewers recommend, and escalate. A therapist concern can only be closed with no action, a warning, a pause on new clients, or escalation. Severity-1 cases can be taken by clinical reviewers, Admins and moderators flagged `heavy` (the heavy-queue rota). Revealing a flagged message is limited to 20 a day per person. A closed case can't be decided again.
- **What a sanction does.** The member gets a notice (`my_notices()`) and their standing changes: a restriction lasts a day and lifts itself (a job every 5 minutes), a suspension pauses the account until a staff member restores it or an appeal succeeds. A reported post is hidden when a sanction is applied.
- **Appeals.** A member appeals a notice from Account standing. The reviewer must be someone other than the person who decided. Upheld keeps it, reduced takes it one step down, overturned removes it.
- **Therapists.** Clinical reviewers and Admins suspend (booked sessions are cancelled), reinstate and lift a pause. A pause is applied by deciding a therapist concern.
- **Held posts.** Posts held for review wait in the Moderation module for a moderator or Admin to approve or remove. If nobody gets to one it goes live after a day (it was 15 seconds before there was a queue).
- **Linking a therapist** is done in the Therapists module (clinical reviewers and Admins): enter the therapist's phone number. If that number has already signed in to Echo it is linked at once; if not, it is remembered and linked the first time it signs in. A number can belong to one therapist, never a staff member. Unlinking needs a reason and cuts off portal access. Both are logged, showing only the last three digits. Migration `20261021000800_link_therapists.sql`, checks in `supabase/tests/link_therapists.sql`.
- **Managing the team** is done in the Roles module, by Admins only. Add a person with a name, a role and a phone number: if the number has already signed in to Echo they become staff at once, otherwise they are listed as waiting and become staff the first time it signs in at the staff sign-in page. Admins can change a role, deactivate or reactivate someone, put a moderator on or off the heavy queue, and cancel an invitation. Nobody can change their own account, and at least 2 Admins must stay active. Adding a person, changing a role and deactivating or reactivating someone need a second Admin (see below); the heavy queue and cancelling an invitation do not. Migration `20261021000900_staff_linking.sql`, checks in `supabase/tests/staff_linking.sql`. The very first Admin is created with a one-time setup code: see below.
- **Two-person approval.** One Admin proposes a team change and a different Admin approves or declines it; only then does it happen. The proposer can withdraw it, one change waits at a time, and it lapses after a day. Everything is checked again when it is approved, including the rule that 2 Admins stay active, and nobody can approve a change to their own account. While there is only one active Admin, changes apply at once (and the log says so), otherwise a new project could never get a second Admin. Migration `20261021001100_two_person_approval.sql`, checks in `supabase/tests/two_person.sql`. The strike policy uses the same rule (next point).
- **Strike policy.** Two numbers, kept on the server: how many days a strike counts for (30 to 365, default 90) and how many hours a restriction lasts (1 to 168, default 24). Sanctions use them when applied, and strikes older than the expiry no longer count towards the ladder. Changing them needs a second Admin to approve, like a team change (withdraw, decline, one at a time, a day to decide; immediate with a single Admin, and logged as such). Migration `20261021001200_strike_policy.sql`.
- **Break-glass.** A moderator or Admin asks to see a reported member's name, phone and place, with a reason (imminent risk, legal, fraud) and a justification of at least 15 characters. A clinical reviewer who is not the requester approves or declines; an approval lasts 30 minutes and a request unanswered for an hour lapses. Only the requester can open the details, only while the approval is live, and each opening is logged. The member is not told. Migration `20261021001300_break_glass.sql`. Checks for both: `supabase/tests/policy_and_glass.sql`.
- **The first Admin** needs no SQL. A one-time setup code (stored only as a hash, in `private.setup`) lets the first person who signs in at the staff page, while nobody is an active Admin, enter their name and the code and become an Admin. The code works once, locks after 5 wrong tries, and stops working as soon as any Admin exists. Migration `20261021001000_first_admin.sql`, checks in `supabase/tests/first_admin.sql` (they need a project with no Admin yet). If every Admin is ever lost, a new code is made by running the `insert into private.setup` statement in the SQL editor with a new hash.
- **Audit.** Every action above writes who, when, what, the target and the reason, in the same transaction as the action. Clinical reviewers and Admins read it; Admins export it.
- **Wellbeing and the heavy-queue rota.** The limits (reveals a day 5 to 100, a break suggested every 2 to 50, a break of 1 to 60 minutes; defaults 20, 8, 10) are set by Admins and enforced on the server. A reviewer's reveal count for the day is counted from the log. Breaks are real: revealing is refused during one. The weekly rota (Monday to Sunday, Nairobi time) is made for this week and next by rotating moderators who were not on it last time to the front; an Admin can change it, but the same people can't work two weeks running. Moderators take Severity-1 cases only if they are on this week's rota (or, before any rota exists, if flagged heavy). Handing a case on and asking for support are logged. Migration `20261021001400_wellbeing.sql`, checks in `supabase/tests/wellbeing.sql`.
- **Analytics.** Counted from real use, not sampled. Chats are deleted after a day, so events are counted when they happen in `private.daily` (registered, verified, profiled, matched, first message, subscribed, renewals, churned, bookings, sessions, ratings, conversations, reports, blocks, refunds) and who was active each day in `private.activity` (sent a message, booked or attended a session). Retention by weekly sign-up cohort, the age-and-language breakdown and time to first review for Severity-1 reports come from those. Groups under 10 are hidden on the server. Installs and directory views are not tracked (the server never sees them) and show as zero. History before this migration is filled in only for things that still exist. Migration `20261021001500_analytics.sql`, checks in `supabase/tests/analytics.sql`.
- **Billing and refunds.** A member can ask for a refund within 14 days of paying (`request_refund`; there is no screen in the member app for it yet). Billing staff and Admins approve or decline. Approving marks the payment refunded and ends the plan's access, or cancels the booked session, and is logged and counted. The money goes back by hand until a payment provider is connected. The module shows pending refunds, failed payments in the last 30 days and paid plans with no subscription (unmatched). Amounts stay hidden while the refund policy (D2) is open. Migration `20261021001600_refunds.sql`, checks in `supabase/tests/refunds.sql`.
- **Resources and help tickets.** The help centre now lives on the server, so staff changes reach members without a release. Departments (owner team, reply target, on or off, order), articles (title, introduction, steps, published or hidden), the urgent-help numbers, the support directory and members' service suggestions are tables; `help_content()` gives the member app the published version and is open to anyone, signed in or not, because urgent help must always be reachable. The app keeps its built-in copy as a fallback and replaces it when the server answers. Members' tickets (`create_ticket`, `reply_ticket`, `resolve_ticket`, `mark_ticket_read`; five new ones a day at most) are read by staff for the departments their role owns (Moderation, Clinical, Billing and Support, or all for Admins), crisis-flagged ones first; staff replies show to the member as unread. Safety and reporting can't be switched off; crisis lines can be edited by clinical reviewers and Admins, editing clears the "checked" mark, and a blank number can't be confirmed; removed numbers are hidden, not deleted. An accepted service suggestion joins the directory hidden until its details are filled in. Migrations `20261021001700_help_content.sql` and `20261021001701_help_seed.sql` (the starting content from the app), checks in `supabase/tests/help.sql`. Staff replies show as "Echo staff", never a named person.
- **Feedback.** The "did you feel heard?" answer is kept after the chat is deleted, one answer per person per chat, and Settings notes are stored with no name or account (a separate counter only limits each person to 10 a day). Staff in Billing and Admin roles see the notes and the shares; the shares appear only once at least 10 people have answered, and the comments members add to an answer are never shown. Reading a note is logged. Migration `20261021001800_feedback_and_applications.sql`, checks in `supabase/tests/feedback_applications.sql`.
- **Therapist applications.** A therapist applies after confirming their phone (no Echo account needed): name, title, gender, licence number and expiry, and two statements. A clinical reviewer or Admin ticks four checks (name, licence with the regulator, expiry, qualification), then approves, asks for more information, or rejects, always with a message. The applicant sees the decision in the app and can send updated details after a request for more information. Approving creates the therapist, links the applicant's number, and leaves them paused and not accepting new clients until they have written a profile and set their hours in the portal. **Uploading documents is not built**: an application records details only, and the screen says so.
- **Configuration** (Admins only, every change logged). The server reads these settings and enforces them in the rules:
  - **Topics:** the list is a table. Renaming carries existing posts and members' chosen topics with it; retiring hides a topic from new posts while old posts and profiles keep it; at least three stay in use.
  - **Feature flags:** with community posting off, posts and comments are refused; with the mood check-in off, mood is refused; with video off, video sessions can't be booked; with disappearing chats off, they can't be proposed.
  - **Plans:** price, whether it is offered, and entitlements (disappearing chats, and a therapy discount). A plan change needs a second Admin to approve, like a team change, and applies at once when there is only one Admin. A plan without the disappearing entitlement can't propose disappearing chats.
  - **Matching:** neighbouring age ranges on or off, topics as a tie-break between people who already share an age range and a language (never a reason to match or not), and the days before the same two people can be matched again.
  - **Limits:** the days between pen-name changes is enforced.
  - The server patches the existing rules in place (`private.patch_function`) so no rule was rewritten by hand. Migration `20261021001900_configuration.sql`, checks in `supabase/tests/configuration.sql`. Members read the settings with `app_config()`, and the app already hides what is switched off.
  - **Grace period:** after a plan ends, peer chats stay readable (read-only) for the configured days, then disappear from the member's list. Therapist chats are not affected.
  - **Therapy discount:** the percentage on the member's current plan comes off a session's price at checkout, and the checkout says so. Directory prices show the list price. The therapist's earnings are unchanged for now, so Echo bears the discount; that is a decision to confirm.

- **Group chats** (members, staff and the therapist who leads). Four weekly rooms (grief, anxiety, recovery, burnout) are created by the server each week, and a clinical reviewer or Admin can schedule or cancel others. The server enforces the plan, the number of places, the ten-minute lobby, the 500-character limit and the contact filter, and it keeps removed people out. Members see only their own room's messages; a moderator's removal hides the person's lines and blocks them from writing or signing up again. The therapist who leads writes through the portal (**Group chats**), from the lobby on. A report keeps the last eight lines with the report and the room is then kept. Migration `20261021002000_group_chats.sql`, checks in `supabase/tests/group_chats.sql`.
  - **Deletion needs one manual step.** Rooms are deleted 24 hours after they end, unless reported. The deleting job is in `supabase/group_chat_cleanup.sql` because the tooling used for migrations does not run deleting SQL. Run it once in the Supabase SQL editor (it asks you to confirm because it deletes). Until then ended rooms are kept.
- **Automated flags** (PRD SAF-6 to SAF-9). The server scores each member message, post and comment (peer chat, community, group chats) with pattern rules, model name `rules-v1`: harassment, threats, self-harm concern, sexual solicitation, scams and money requests, hate speech. A match writes a flag (category, confidence, model, a message reference, never the text) and opens a case in the queue as an "AI flag" with a snapshot of the last lines, which staff read like any report. At most one open flag per member and category an hour. A flag never acts on its own. When staff decide the case, the flag is **upheld** (any action but dismiss) or **overturned** (dismissed); escalating leaves it open. Moderation and Analytics read the log and the precision, overturn rate and review time from `admin_flags()`. Migration `20261021002100_ai_flags.sql`, checks in `supabase/tests/ai_flags.sql`. Rules are rows in `private.flag_rules`, so they can be tuned in SQL. A real model can write to the same log under its own model name; no message text leaves Supabase.
  - **Limits:** attempts blocked by the contact filter are not logged as flags; there is no screen to edit the rules yet; self-harm matches open a high-severity case for a person to look at, and the right action is usually to dismiss it or escalate it, not a sanction.
- **Therapist appeals.** The server now keeps a record of each decision in force against a therapist: a warning or a pause on new clients (from a clinical reviewer closing a concern case) and a suspension. Lifting a pause or reinstating clears it. The therapist sees the most serious one in the portal and can appeal it (20 to 2,000 characters, one open appeal at a time). A **different** clinical reviewer or Admin decides: uphold (nothing changes), reduce one step (suspension becomes a pause, a pause becomes a warning, a warning is cleared) or overturn (everything is removed). The therapist sees the explanation. Moderators and billing staff can't read or decide them. Migration `20261021002200_therapist_appeals.sql`, checks in `supabase/tests/therapist_appeals.sql`. A decision made before this migration has no record, so it can't be appealed from the portal.
- **Licence expiry.** A daily job (`echo-tick-licences`, 08:00 EAT) records a reminder for each therapist at 60, 30 and 7 days before the licence runs out and once it has. The therapist sees the stage and a renewal form on Profile; an expired licence hides them from the directory and from booking (existing sessions are not cancelled). They send the new licence number and expiry; a clinical reviewer or Admin approves or rejects with a note, and approval extends the licence. Clinical reviewers and Admins see every licence and any waiting renewal on the Therapists screen, and the Overview counts the ones in a stage. Migration `20261021002300_licence_reminders.sql`, checks in `supabase/tests/licence_reminders.sql`.
  - **Limits:** reminders are shown in the portal only (no SMS or email provider yet), and the renewal is a number and a date, not an uploaded certificate (Q-38).
- **Refund requests, member side.** The member app has a **Payments and refunds** screen (from My plan) listing the last 20 payments. - **Ticket reply notifications.** When staff reply to a help ticket, the member gets an in-app note ("Help has replied") that opens the ticket. It appears once per reply, while the reply is unread, is checked about every 30 seconds while the app is open, and respects the member's notification settings and quiet hours. The note shows no content of the reply. This is an in-app note, not a push to a locked phone; real push needs a provider.
- **Account deletion needs one manual step.** Run `supabase/account_deletion_cleanup.sql` once in the Supabase SQL editor (it asks you to confirm because it deletes). An hourly job then deletes accounts whose deletion was requested more than the holding period ago, with everything that belongs to them. Staff and therapist accounts are never deleted this way. Until you run it, requested deletions stay on hold. Posts the member chose to keep are **not** kept yet: they go with the account (see Q-44).
- **Documents for applications and renewals.** An applicant uploads their licence, national ID and qualification certificate (PDF, JPEG or PNG, up to 5 MB each, at most 12 files) from the application form, and a therapist attaches the renewed certificate to a licence renewal. Files go to a **private storage bucket** (`applications`), one folder per person; they can add to and read only their own folder. ## Setup

1. In the Supabase dashboard open **Authentication → Providers → Phone** and turn it on. Under **Test phone numbers** add the numbers you will try, for example `254712345678=123456`. Test numbers send no text and always accept their fixed code. (A real SMS provider comes later.)
2. In `mobile/`, copy `.env.example` to `.env`. It points at this project and holds the public key, which is meant to be public.
3. `npx expo start`. With `EXPO_PUBLIC_BACKEND=supabase` the app signs up and in against the real backend. With it unset the app uses the on-device mock, which is what the unit tests and browser flows use.

## Re-running the checks

Paste `supabase/tests/foundation.sql` into the SQL editor (or run it through the MCP `execute_sql`). It always rolls back. The message `ALL FOUNDATION CHECKS PASSED` means success; anything else lists the failures.
