Echo — Wireframes: Help centre

Draft v0.1 · low-fidelity · follows screen-flows.md §9 and PRD §6.10 (HLP-1 to HLP-3). Uses the Home / Chats / Forum / Therapists / Help tab layout (D22). Crisis numbers, organisation names, response times and amounts are placeholders and must be verified before launch. Pink markers match the numbered notes beside each group. Not committed.

Principles

  • Urgent help comes first. It is the top card on Help home and is also the orange pill on every screen. It is never behind a paywall.
  • Organised by department, so each topic has an owner and a contact route (Support, Billing, Safety, Clinical, Tech).
  • Plain language, English and Kiswahili. Short articles, step-by-step where possible.
  • Private by design. Staff see the pen name only; tickets are text only; messages that mention risk are routed to the crisis queue, whatever department they were sent to.

Decisions and gaps

  • Response times: every “within ___ hours” is a placeholder. Set per department and staff accordingly.
  • Live chat support: not drawn. Do you want live help for urgent billing or technical problems, or tickets only?
  • Screenshots in tickets: drawn as text only. Technical support often needs a screenshot; if allowed, it must avoid personal information.
  • Kiswahili and Sheng: who writes and reviews the translations?
  • Support directory: who curates it, how often it is re-verified, and whether Echo can list organisations it hasn’t partnered with.
  • Not drawn: admin screens for editing help content and answering tickets.
