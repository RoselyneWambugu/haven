Echo — Wireframes: Therapist directory, booking and sessions (member side)

Draft v0.1 · low-fidelity · follows screen-flows.md §5 and PRD §6.5. These sit between the therapist profile and booking screens (4a, 4b), checkout (13a) and the pre-session check (4c) already drawn. Prices are placeholders (___). Pink markers match the numbered notes beside each group. Not committed.

Principles

  • Clinical and peer are visibly different. Therapy has its own tab, its own price per session and its own notices.
  • Fair discovery. Default order is by next available time; nobody pays to rank higher (D38).
  • Members stay anonymous; therapists are public. Calls show the member as a pen name and avatar, with camera off by default.
  • Honest policies, stated early: cancellation windows and charges appear before payment and again when changing a booking.
  • Works on poor networks: voice is the low-data option, and calls fall back from video to voice to chat.

Decisions and gaps

  • D37: therapist gender and other preference filters, and the “Help me choose” questionnaire. Drawn as optional.
  • D38: ranking and fairness. Default “next available”; no paid placement; how ties and new therapists are treated.
  • D39: cancellation window, late-change and no-show charges, and what happens if the therapist cancels or is late.
  • D40: call technology, join window (10 minutes drawn), fallback rules and data use. Needs a provider that works well on mobile data in Kenya.
  • Waitlists: who is notified first, and for how long a freed slot is held.
  • Not yet drawn: returning-user login, admin analytics and configuration.
