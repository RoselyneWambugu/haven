Echo, in colour and type.

A visual direction drawn from your three references: soft greens on pale paper, a gentle serif, large rounded shapes and quiet botanical forms. The one idea we added is the ripple, because an echo is a sound that spreads and fades.

Kept from your references

  • Pale green paper, deep forest panels, olive and sage between.
  • A soft serif for headlines and a plain sans for reading.
  • Pill buttons, arch windows and very rounded cards.
  • Botanical shapes and a warm sun, drawn rather than photographed.

Chosen for Echo

  • The ripple marks matching, calls, avatars and the landing page.
  • Two voices: a statement in roman, a reply in italic, like speaking and listening.
  • One warm colour only, and only where attention is needed.
  • A hyperlegible body face for tired eyes and small phones.

Left out on purpose

  • Stock photography of people. Drawn scenes keep it calm and keep faces private.
  • Streaks, scores and badges that pressure people.
  • Capital-letter labels above every heading.
  • The same shadow and corner radius on every card.

The ripple, in three places

Waiting is a ripple. A call is a ripple around a voice. Calm is a ripple that breathes. It always moves slowly, and stops for anyone who prefers reduced motion.

Twelve colours, two typefaces, three corner shapes. The palette stays green and quiet, with one warm colour that means “this needs you”.

Notes and decisions

Accessibility

  • Body text meets AA everywhere; most pairs reach AAA.
  • Touch targets are 44 pixels or more; focus is a thick visible ring.
  • Colour never carries meaning alone: urgent help also says “Urgent help”.
  • Ripples and breathing stop under reduced motion.
  • Text should grow to 200% without breaking; Kiswahili and Sheng labels are often longer than English.

Open design decisions

  • D45: illustrated scenes or real photography for the landing page. Photos need consent and licensing.
  • D46: a dark mode. The palette supports it; the fern panels already hint at it.
  • D47: the logo. The wordmark and ripple mark here are a sketch, not final.
  • D48: whether the mood check-in stays (see D25).
  • D49: how real therapist photographs are framed, and what happens if a therapist prefers none.

Fonts and the ripple animation are bundled in this file. Nothing loads from the internet.
