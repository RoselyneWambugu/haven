  1. 1Overflow menu opens the actions in 9b.
  2. 2One reaction (“Support”), a comment count and a Report entry. There are no downvotes. Authors see counts, not who reacted.
  3. 3Therapist comments are highlighted, show the real name and a verified badge, and carry a standing line: general information, not treatment. Tapping opens their public profile (and from there, booking).
  4. 4Comments with contact details or links never appear. The author sees a neutral placeholder; the commenter is told why when they try to post.
  5. 5Composer applies the same checks as chat (links, phone numbers, addresses) plus identifying-detail warnings. There is no “message this person” button anywhere in the forum.
  6. 6Report and block are always in the menu. Report on a post snapshots it into a case.
  7. 7Authors can delete any time. Edits are limited to 15 minutes to prevent changing a post after replies; deletion doesn’t remove snapshots held in an open case.
  8. 8First posts from new accounts are held for review. The author sees a clear status and expected wait.
  9. 9Authors of posts that signal distress get a gentle prompt to talk now. Others see nothing different.

Design notes and open questions

  • No dating mechanics: no photos, no profile pages for members, no follow, no DM from a post. Tapping a pen name does nothing except offer mute and block.
  • Therapist solicitation: therapist comments may link to their in-app profile, nothing else (PRD FRM-8).
  • Reactions: the draft uses a single “Support” reaction. Do you also want “I relate”? More reactions add moderation and visual noise.
  • Visibility: members only, not indexed by search engines (D20). Free accounts can read and post.
  • Edit window: 15 minutes is my default. Easy to change.
  • Sensitive posts: a collapsed “content note” veil for self-harm or abuse topics is not drawn here. Decide with the clinical lead whether to add it.
