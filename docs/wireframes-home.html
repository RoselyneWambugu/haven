Navigation change proposed here

Earlier flows treated “Chats” as the home screen. These wireframes split them: Home is a calm landing page; Chats is the full conversation list. To keep five tabs, Me moves to the avatar at the top-left of every screen: Home · Chats · Forum · Therapists · Help. Needs your decision (D22).

Design notes

  • No gender, photos, distances or “online now” indicators, to keep the app from feeling like dating.
  • Unread counts use orange sparingly; avoid red except for block/destructive actions.
  • Push notifications and lock-screen previews stay content-free by default.
  • Decision D22: confirm the tab change (Me moves to the top-left avatar). The earlier wireframes still show the old five tabs.
