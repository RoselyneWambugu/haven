Open decisions that affect these screens

  • D2: prices, daily/monthly/yearly entitlements, free tier, refund policy. All shown as placeholders.
  • Grace period: 7 days of read-only chats after lapse is a placeholder.
  • Auto-renew mechanics: mobile-money providers vary in recurring-payment support; the toggle may mean “remind me” for some methods. Confirm with the payment provider.
  • Therapy bundling: do any plans include therapy credits or discounts? Drawn as a discount line only.
  • App stores: if distributed via Google Play or the App Store, store billing rules may require their payment flow for digital subscriptions. Check before building.
  • Taxes: whether prices are shown inclusive of VAT.
