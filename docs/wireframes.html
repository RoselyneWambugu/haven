Design notes

Things these wireframes assume that you may want to change.

  • Gender, bio, photos and distance are deliberately absent from every member-facing screen to keep the app from feeling like dating.
  • The “Need urgent help?” entry is present in the top bar on nearly every screen; crisis numbers shown must be verified by the clinical team.
  • Prices, copy and therapist details are placeholders.
  • Navigation is five bottom tabs (Chats, Forum, Therapists, Help, Me), per the screen-flows document.
