Open questions

  • D23: web portal first, or a mobile app for therapists too? Drawn as web.
  • Caseload and safety: should the platform enforce maximum active clients and a minimum gap between a therapist’s sessions?
  • Forum: the portal nav includes “Forum”; therapist comments follow PRD FRM-2 and FRM-8 (general information, no soliciting).
  • Peer supervision / support for therapists: not drawn; consider a channel for therapists to consult the clinical lead.
  • On-call clinical lead: the concern flow assumes one exists, with response targets. Needs staffing before the pilot.
